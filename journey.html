Chapter 02 · Oct 2023 to Jan 2024

Building and research

A hackathon project, then my first paper and its publication.

2023

  1. Oct 2023

    Project · Override hackathon

    Built SoilSense in 24 hours

    Neighbors kept losing their plants to poor soil. At the Override hackathon, our team built a platform that turns city food waste into compost, with a nutrient report for every batch and an AI chatbot for customers.

    Read about SoilSense

  2. Nov 2023

    Research · Belagavi, India

    First paper at an IEEE conference

    My research on HVSNA, a hybrid attack on RPL-based IoT networks, appeared at the 2023 IEEE NKCon conference in Belagavi, India.

    Read about HVSNA

  3. Jan 2024

    Research · IEEE Xplore

    Published in IEEE Xplore

    The HVSNA paper was added to IEEE Xplore, making the work citable by other researchers.

Teaching and industry

Leading labs, my first industry role, a second paper, and graduation.

2024

  1. Aug 2024

    Teaching · PES University

    Became a teaching assistant for Machine Learning, leading labs for more than 120 students and mentoring teams through complete projects. Explaining ideas to others sharpened my own.

  2. Jan 2025

    Industry · Aon, Bangalore

    First industry role at Aon

    As a data engineering intern, I optimized production SQL pipelines over more than 10 million records, cutting processing time by 98% and saving the team 70+ hours a month.

  3. Jun 2025

    Graduated from PES University. The same month, my paper on real-time speech generation from Indian Sign Language was part of ICCEE 2025 in Singapore.

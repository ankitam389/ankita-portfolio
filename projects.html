Real-Time Speech Generation of Regional Languages Using Indian Sign Language

ICCEE 2025, 18th International Conference on Computer and Electrical Engineering, Singapore. Published in Advances in Transdisciplinary Engineering, September 2025.

Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, GRU, Flask, speech synthesis

Deaf and hard-of-hearing signers often need an interpreter to talk with people who do not know sign language, and most tools ignore regional languages entirely. This system watches a webcam, recognizes Indian Sign Language as it is signed, and speaks the result aloud in the listener's language.

  • Extracts 84 hand keypoints per frame with MediaPipe and trains a GRU sequence model on 94 gesture labels with 240 videos each.
  • Reaches 89.45% accuracy on the held-out test set.
  • A Flask service chains recognition, translation, and speech synthesis across six languages, with end-to-end latency under 300 ms.

HVSNA: An Advanced Hybrid Attack on RPL-Based Low-Power Wireless Networks

2023 IEEE North Karnataka Subsection Flagship International Conference (NKCon), Belagavi, India, November 2023. In IEEE Xplore since January 2024.

Contiki OS, Cooja, C, RPL

RPL is the routing protocol that many IoT sensor networks rely on. I designed a new attack that combines two known weaknesses, the version-number attack and the sinkhole attack, and measured how badly the combination damages a network.

  • Modified the RPL protocol stack in Contiki OS to implement the hybrid attack.
  • Ran large-scale simulations with 20 to 50 wireless nodes, measuring latency, power consumption, network stability, and resilience under attack.
  • The interactive network on my home page shows a simplified sinkhole in action.

BioScope: biomedical question answering

Problem

Language models can sound confident about medicine while being wrong. BioScope answers biomedical questions only from retrieved literature and shows the citation behind every claim.

Technologies

Python, RAG, UMLS, TF-IDF, scikit-learn, Gemini

Approach and results

  • Combines semantic retrieval with UMLS entity linking, so synonyms of the same medical concept retrieve the same evidence.
  • Routes each question with a 7-class classifier trained on 700 labeled examples; LinearSVC reached 97.14% cross-validation accuracy.
  • Generates citation-backed answers with an LLM, improving how well each answer is grounded in its sources.

SoilSense: turning city food waste into compost

Built in 24 hours at the Override hackathon, 2023.

Problem

It started with neighbors in our apartment complex whose plants kept dying, often because the soil lacked nutrients, and the soil they needed was not sold in stores or online. Meanwhile, cities throw away enormous amounts of organic waste. SoilSense connects the two with a web platform that follows waste from collection to a bag of compost for urban gardeners.

Technologies

Python, MySQL, OpenAI API

Approach and results

  • Analyzes the contents of collected waste and generates a full nutrient panel for each batch, covering nitrogen, carbon, and phosphorus.
  • Tracks each batch through blending to target nutrient ratios, packaging, and sales, with automated data processing.
  • Validates data at every step so errors are caught before they reach reports or sales records.
  • A horticulture chatbot built on the OpenAI API answers customer questions about each product's contents, usage, and benefits, using the platform's own product and nutrient data.
